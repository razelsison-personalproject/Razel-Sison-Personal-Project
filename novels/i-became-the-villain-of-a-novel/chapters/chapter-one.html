CHAPTER 1 • I BECAME THE VILLAIN OF A NOVEL

Crossing Paths with Danger

← PREV INDEX NEXT →

The smell of incense and heavy velvet was the first sign that something had gone terribly wrong.

Opening my eyes, I didn't see the familiar ceiling of my apartment or hear the hum of city traffic below. Instead, golden chandeliers hung overhead from vaulted stone arches, casting flickering shadows across a room five times the size of my entire home.

I tried to sit up, but my body felt strange—heavy, clad in dark silk tailored with silver thread. When I looked down at my hands, they were pale, unblemished, and adorned with heavy onyx rings.

"Young Lady, Lord Adrian... the Duke requests your presence at the courtyard."

The voice came from the doorway. A trembling attendant wearing a crest I had only ever seen illustrated on a screen. Adrian von Valerius. The notorious villain destined to be executed in Chapter 42.
