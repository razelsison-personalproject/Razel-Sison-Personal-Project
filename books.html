ON-GOING Dark Fantasy 2022

I BECAME THE VILLAIN OF A NOVEL

When I woke up this morning, I found myself in a strange room, unfamiliar bed, strangers I've never met, and a body that wasn't mine.

The strangers wore a maid outfit, and these people were calling me by the name Nasrin Urduja. This name reminded me of the novel I read before coming into this place. In that novel, Nasrin Urduja was the name of the villain, and her only ending in the book was death.

It's strange, and I don't know how it all happened, but right now, I am Nasrin Urduja, the villain of the novel I've read before waking up to this unfamiliar world.

In order to survive, I plan to stay away from the main characters of the novel and live my new life as a hermit on a far away forest, but as soon as I turn around, I saw the friendly smiling face of the protagonist.

I'm doomed!

ON-GOING Adventure Fantasy 2022

LIVING A LIFE OF A SALTED FISH

It was the middle of the night; I encountered a robbery. I fought back, struggling, kicking the robber with my heels, and just like what happens in most cases, I was stabbed multiple times and died from blood loss.

The next thing I knew; I found myself waking up in an unfamiliar room.

Eventually, I found that everything is not mine, including my body. The face is similar to mine, but everything else is eccentric. Especially the woman who seems to have abducted me.

Later on, I discovered that I transmigrated in the world of the Lily Novel I once read, and in the book, I have a similar name as the heroine, Nisha Sullivan. And the one who abducted me is the villainess, Ruhee Bellerose.

The plot of the Lily Novel is simple; Ruhee will repeatedly imprison Nisha multipletimes, and Nisha will always end up being saved by the female-partner, Cleopatra Denisovan, until Nisha and Cleopatra become inseparable partners.

I'm not sure how things went, but in the end, Cleopatra and Nisha got happily ever after. Ruhee, on the other hand, got exiled from the kingdom, living a life even poorer than a beggar.

A sigh came out of my mouth. To be honest, I don't care about any of these characters. I just want to live an easy life like a salted fish. After all, I worked hard day and night in my past life, and I never experienced having an easy life.

That's why! My only goal is to live a life doing nothing but sleep, eat, rest, and have fun!

After a week of staying in Ruhee's mansion, I discovered that it wasn't so bad to stay as her prisoner. I get a meal three times a day, and there are maids around me who are always accompanying me wherever I go in the villa.

So when Cleopatra came to me, trying to save me like a hero of justice; I decisively ran away, hiding behind Ruhee.

"Help, someone is abducting me!"

I TRANSMIGRATED TO A SCI-FI WORLD AND BECAME A MOTHER!

Asherah woke up, finding herself inside the operating room in the hospital.

Having no recollections of how Asherah got herself there; Asherah felt baffled.

"One more push, ma'am," said the nurse, standing beside her bed.

Asherah glanced at the nurse, confused. "What do you mean?"

"Just one more push and the child will finally come out, ma'am," the nurse said again.

Asherah widened her eyes in utter shock.

"But I'm gay?!" Asherah muttered, puzzled.
