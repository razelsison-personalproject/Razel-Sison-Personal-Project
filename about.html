About

Flos Vloom
Novels Published 4
Languages Translated 11
Years Writing 14

Flos Vloom is the author of four novels of literary and gothic fiction, published in eleven languages and awarded the Hartley Prize shortlist for her debut. She lives on the coast of Cornwall, where she keeps two cats, too many unread books, and a view she can't stop writing about.

She began writing seriously at twenty-two, after two years studying medieval cartography at the University of Edinburgh — a detour that shaped everything that followed. Her fiction is preoccupied with what maps leave out, what memory distorts, and what history refuses to give back.

✦
"I am interested in the border between what happened and what we can bear to remember. Fiction lives there."
✦

Before publishing fiction, she worked as a researcher for a documentary production company and briefly — memorably — as the night-shift archivist for a private library in Vienna. That library appears, in various forms, in all four of her novels.

Her work has been reviewed in The Guardian, The New York Times Book Review, The Paris Review, and The Times Literary Supplement. She has spoken at the Edinburgh International Book Festival, Hay Festival, and the Ubud Writers & Readers Festival.

She is currently completing her fifth novel.
