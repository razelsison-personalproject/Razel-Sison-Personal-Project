Flos Vloom
Novels Published in Wattpad 10
Language English

Flos Vloom is the author of literary sapphic fiction novels, published through Wattpad. She lives on a tiny dust somewhere in the cosmos where intelligent species calls it Earth. In her cave, she was enslaved by cats and has been living a life of a hermit.

She began writing seriously at twenty-two, after a contagious virus spread around the world causing millions to stay at home, including her.

✦
"I believed that every imagination deserved to be written and shared."
✦

Before publishing fiction, she was studying at a University of who-knows-where. And when the pandemic happened, she ultimately decided to dropped from school and start making a name for herself in the literary world.

Her work has been reviewed by users in Wattpad.

She is currently completing all of her novels.
